Where the Reader Waited

When a scene's image arrives after its prose has finished revealing, the reader is looking at the previous scene's atmosphere — or, on the first scene of a session, at empty dark. Buckets count those moments by lag. Anything under 100ms is imperceptible; anything past 500ms is felt.

The Wire They Came Through

Effective network class — the four-bucket speed estimate (4G / 3G / 2G / slow 2G) with average downlink and round-trip when reported. This is observed speed, not physical link: a slow wifi shows as 3g. Safari does not expose the API; those sessions show as unknown. The physical wifi-vs-cellular distinction is hidden by most browsers as anti-fingerprinting and so isn't shown here.
